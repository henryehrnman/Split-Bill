Product schema as follows:
  1) initial funding and account setup:
    i) Members make accounts and an individual would create a payment group. Within the creation of the payment group, this individual would decide on a budget, which includes a group budget, and the period of time that budget is for (e.g. $300 every 2 weeks). They would then invite other accounts to join their group by typing in their userid, email, or phone number in the app, and selecting the profile that comes up. When accepting an invite to a group, the details of the group are displayed to the user who will then agree and join or disagree. A group fund is created, and each member is issued a visa card that is connected to that account. At the time of group creation, each member contribues an equal share of the agreed upon budget (outlined in 3.ii). Any member can add a card assigned to the group to their e-wallet and pay for group purchases. Each member's individual balance and spend is tracked seperately, but all funds are allocated to the same group fund (outlined in 2, 5.ii).
    ii) At any time, users can propose changing the buying power of the card, either increasing or decreasing it. Increasing requires each user to deposit enough money to cover their equal split for the next period, wheras decreasing keeps balances the same, keeping any surplus.
    iii) At the beginning of each new period, users either manually add funds or are automatically charged the amount required to top them up to their equal split of the budget. (e.g. if you had a group budget of $300 for 3 people, which is a $100 contribution per person, and had $40 in your account at the end of the period, you would only be charged $60 at the beginning of the next period).

  2) purchases:
    i) On purchase, funds are withdrawn from the group fund, and the app tentitaviely proposes the set default split for the charge (The current pending splits being displayed on the main fund screen under the group fund amount, it would default to whatever the users have chosen as the automatic split (defaults to equally across members, can be changed)), from there, each user goes into the app some time before the group's next purchase, and can modify, propose, and accept the split. 
    ii) Splits can be itemized, by percent, or by dollar amount. Splits must always cover the entire bill. We do not round or drop cents, and all charge must be allocated before a split is accepted.
    iii) Charge splits are iterative, meaning the first split proposed by a given user is shown to the rest of the group, and then each other user can choose to accept or modify, with the requirement being each user involved in a transaction must agree to the split (if a user is not part of a transaction, say in a case where they did not go to a group dinner, other members can accept a split that does not allocate any spend to that user without needing their approval). 
    iv) Users may spend more than their individual buying power, as groups operate on a foundation of trust between members. However, once a users balance hits or falls below 0 (outlined in 3.i-iii), their buying power is temporarily restricted until they add funds.
    v) Each split must be accepted before the next purchase is made, we do not allow for multiple floating charges, as that could allow users to spend greatly over their own capability. Once the split has been accepted and balances updated, the card is ready for another purchase.

  3) after a split is accepted:
    i) Once a split has been accepted, each users tracked balance is decreased by the agreed upon amount. Buying power of the card is decreased accordingly. Individual balances can always be adjusted, with users freely able to add funds, and able to withdraw funds whenever there is not a split pending.
    ii) If a user's periodly top-up fails or they choose not to add more funds, their balance reflects that, meaning the group buying power (and their individual spend approval) is reduced to their current balance.
    iii) If a user spends their entirely periodly balance, they are restricted from using the group fund until the next budget period, or until they add more funds to their accound. If a user goes negative after a split is agreed upon, their connected bank is automatically charged to make up the difference and bring their balance to 0, if this charge fails, their account is temporarily suspended until the debt is repaid. Importantly, this debt is on the other members of the group, and the group buying power will fall below the other member's balances until rectified. 

  4) disputes:
    i) The product mainly operates on a system of trust, it is expected that members within a group are on a good basis with each other (roommates, spouses, friends, coworkers, etc.), and the product serves to streamline the payment process for group purchases. Any disputes, fraud, or other split issues can not be solely handled by the product itself, and in the result of a major lasting issue, the product keeps the right to resolve the dispute as they see fit, and disband the payment group. If users cannot agree upon a split within a week of a charge, the account is frozen, and the charge is escalated to a dispute center (not implementing in this scope, just as a hypothetical fallback).
    ii) User chargebacks after spend also results in freezing the account, automatically escalating to the dispute center as potential fraud, where support chooses how to handle the member and group.
    iii) There are a multitude of other edge cases and issues that we will choose to ignore here. We're working on an idea & MVP for a hackathon. We want this succesfully built and fleshed out, but it does not need to be an enterprise standard financial system from day one.

  5) features and tracking
    i) At time of purchase, users can choose to upload a picture of the reciept, which the product will scan and store. When a receipt is successfully processed, the itemized bill will be added to the split, and users can choose to add items to their own charge (with the total tax on the receipt defaulting to being allocated per member by amount on items taken responsability for). Items can also be split between users, or edited if the product processed something wrong, and users may still choose to split based on their own dollar amounts or percentages if they wish. For an itemized split, each user will take responsability for items and then lock in their selection. Other users can then see their selections, and choose to suggest an edit to another users items if they wish, or simply choose their own items and continue. Once all items are accounted for and there are no pending edits, the itemized split is confirmed and charged.
    ii) Each charge and the agreed upon split will be stored and tracked in a seperate tab, so each user can see the monthly and total spend for the group, along with the charges and their details. Each user can also see their own balance, spend, and charges they were involved in within their own profile at any time.
